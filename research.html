PHAIMS

PHysics-aware AI for iMaging Science: from space to atoms

PHAIMS combines artificial intelligence with physics-based models to address imaging problems where measurements have complex relationships with physical quantities and reference data are scarce. It studies multispectral and hyperspectral imaging, thermal imaging and tomography, with applications in remote sensing, biomedical imaging and materials science. The project aims to develop interpretable AI methods that use computational resources efficiently.
